Our story

From one oven to two-forty.

Pizza Max started as a single wood-fired oven and a stubborn belief that fast food didn't have to mean forgettable food. Here's how we got here.

2011

The first oven

One location, one recipe, hand-stretched dough baked in a reclaimed brick oven.

2015

Sourcing gets serious

Partnered directly with regional farms for tomatoes, basil, and fresh mozzarella.

2019

Built for delivery

Redesigned kitchens around a 12-minute bake-to-box target without cutting a single ingredient.

2024

240 locations

Live order tracking, a loyalty program, and a pizza builder that never runs out of combinations.
