Our Story

Where fire, craft & hospitality meet

Royal Taste began as a single charcoal grill and a promise: every plate leaves the kitchen exactly as our chefs would serve their own family. Fifteen years on, that promise still shapes every dish, from the wood-fired ovens to the sommelier's cellar.

Today our kitchen brigade of eighteen chefs sources daily from local farms and fisheries, building menus around what's freshest — not what's easiest.

About Royal Taste

Our Story, Our Mission

Fifteen years of fire, craft and hospitality — built one plate, one evening, one guest at a time.

Since 2009

Built around the open flame

Royal Taste opened its doors with one belief: the best hospitality happens where guests can see the fire, smell the smoke, and trust the hands preparing their meal. That philosophy still drives every decision, from our open kitchen to the way our servers describe each dish.

  • Locally sourced, seasonally driven menus
  • Hand-selected wine & cocktail pairings
  • Michelin-trained kitchen brigade
